Safe Badge

A browser extension that lets Twitch viewers know you've disabled Gen AI training on your stream.

Recent Twitch changes enabled Gen AI training by default. Viewers have no way of disabling this on streams they're watching. In fact, there's no way to even know it's enabled on a stream. Safe Badge is the best solution I can think of.

Safe Badge operates on an honor system.

By confirming, you attest that you have disabled Gen AI training on your account.

Why does this require my email?

It actually doesn't. Twitch makes it seem like it does, but hear me out.

The Twitch API requires an OAuth scope to authenticate you. The narrowest scope I can choose is openid, which is part of OpenID Connect. OpenID Connect can include your email address, profile pic, etc.

However, OpenID requires these pieces of info to be requested through what are called claims. By default, none of that information is actually claimed, so it's never sent to my server. The OAuth URL would need to include &claims={jsonObject} to explicitly request your email address.

You can verify what I'm telling you by checking the official Twitch API documentation on OpenID here.
